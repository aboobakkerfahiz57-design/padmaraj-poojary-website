About Padmaraj

A journey rooted in law, service and public life.

Padmaraj R. Poojary, also known as Padmaraj Ramaiah, is an advocate, politician and social worker based in Mangaluru, Karnataka.

He completed his B.A. at Sacred Heart College, Madanthyar, and earned his law degree from SDM Law College, Mangaluru. He is an Advocate and Notary Public.

He originally hails from Balur in Moodigere taluk of Chikkamagaluru district and has long been settled in Mangaluru.
