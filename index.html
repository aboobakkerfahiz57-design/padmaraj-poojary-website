About Padmaraj

A journey rooted in law, service and public life.

Padmaraj R. Poojary, also known as Padmaraj Ramaiah, is an advocate, politician and social worker based in Mangaluru, Karnataka.

His professional journey spans legal practice, community initiatives, social service and public life. He has also been associated with community and cultural activities in coastal Karnataka.

He originally hails from Balur in Moodigere taluk of Chikkamagaluru district and has long been settled in Mangaluru.

1995 Beginning of legal practice
B.A. Sacred Heart College, Madanthyar
LL.B. SDM Law College, Mangaluru
